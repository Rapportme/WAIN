How the points work

Every goal carries points — 10 by default, set when you create it. When a goal is marked hit (or reward given), everyone on that goal gets its points. If no one was picked, the whole team gets them.

The leaderboard adds up points for the period you choose. Use it for recognition at the end of each month or quarter — e.g. the top scorer picks the team outing, or points convert to a bonus (say ₹50 a point).

Give bigger or harder goals more points (25–50) and small internal ones fewer (5–10). Missed or cancelled goals earn nothing.

Accounts

Client Priorities & Profitability
—
How priority is worked out

Each client gets a score out of 100: revenue band up to 30 (A = the clients making up the top 70% of paid revenue in the last 12 months, B = the next 20%, C = the rest), margin up to 20 (revenue minus costs logged to the client, its cost center or its projects: High ≥ 40%, OK 15–40%, Thin 0–15%, Loss), payment health up to 15 (On time, Slow = paid 7+ days late on average, Overdue), segment up to 20 (Strategic, Growth, Partner/Retainer, One-off) and tier up to 15 (Premium to Starter). 65+ is P1, 40–64 is P2, below 40 is P3; paused or churned clients are always P3. Projects take 60% of their client's score, plus 25 if billable (12 pitch, 5 internal) and 15 if due within two weeks. Pin a different priority with a reason whenever the numbers miss something. The team sees only the P1/P2/P3 badge, never the money.
